Design brief · Draft 1

Nations Wars

A Unity RTS in the spirit of Red Alert. We build Skirmish mode first: you against AI opponents on one map, with a choice of five nations. Each nation has its own buildings, units, weapons and a superweapon. Players harvest ore, capture oil and manage a power grid.

The battle scenes below are live 3D mockups built in the browser to compare directions. They show the target look, not captures from Unity.

Visual direction

Live 3D scenes rendered in the browser, with dynamic lighting, soft shadows, glow effects and animated units. All three use a modern RTS HUD: resources across the top, and the minimap, selected units and build queue along the bottom.

A · Cold War alt-history

RecommendedClosest to Red Alert

Pros

  • Instantly readable genre. Players know the rules on sight.
  • Bright, saturated palette keeps units readable at small size.
  • Tesla and superweapon tech gives wild units room to fit.

Cons

  • Close to a well-known franchise, so we need our own humor and silhouettes.

B · Near-future 2070

Sci-fi

Pros

  • Glow and emissive materials look great in URP with little art budget.
  • Easy to justify drones, shields and hover units.

Cons

  • Dark ground plus glowing units can make readability suffer in big fights.
  • Further from the classic Red Alert feel you asked for.

C · Winter dieselpunk

Grounded

Pros

  • Distinct mood, and snow gives natural contrast for units.
  • Rivets, smoke and brass UI give a strong identity.

Cons

  • A muted palette makes teams harder to tell apart without strong team colors.
  • Air power and superweapons feel less at home.
Mode 1

Skirmish mode

Pick a map, fill 2–8 slots with players or AI, choose a nation for each, and play until one side remains.

Core game loop

  • Deploy the MCV into a Construction Yard.
  • Build power, then a refinery (comes with a free harvester), then barracks and a war factory.
  • Scout, capture oil derricks and expand to new ore fields.
  • Tech up, build an army, raid harvesters, defend your power.
  • Charge the superweapon and break the enemy base.

RTS controls (must-have)

  • Box select, double-click to select all of one type, Ctrl+1–9 control groups.
  • Right-click to move or attack. Attack-move, force-fire, waypoints, guard.
  • Stances: aggressive, guard, hold fire.
  • Sidebar build queue with repeat build and rally points.
  • Click the minimap to jump the camera. Edge-pan and zoom.
5 factions

Nations

Every nation shares a common skeleton (HQ, power, barracks, factory, refinery), so the game stays learnable. Each one then gets its own passive, unique buildings, a 7-unit roster and a superweapon.

35 units

Armies in 3D

Every nation fields 7 units: three infantry, ground vehicles, and aircraft. Each line goes infantry first, then vehicles, then air. The models are rough concept art that show silhouettes and style, and they turn slowly.

Economy · 3 options

Resources

1 · Classic ore

One currency. Harvesters mine ore and gems and bring them to a refinery. Power is a separate constraint.

Simplest and most faithful to Red Alert. There are few reasons to fight over the map beyond ore fields.

Recommended

2 · Ore + Oil + Power

Ore is harvested as in Red Alert. Neutral oil derricks are captured with infantry and give a steady trickle of credits. Power gates what runs.

Adds map-control objectives, rewards raiding, and gives Sand Scorpion an economic identity.

3 · Territory control

Capture sectors on the map. Each sector adds income per minute. There are no harvesters.

Aggressive and modern (Company of Heroes style), but it loses the harvester-raiding feel of Red Alert.

Weapons

Damage vs armor

Every weapon has a warhead type and every unit has an armor class. Damage is multiplied by this table, which gives counters without special cases. Values are in percent.

Veterancy: a unit earns XP equal to the cost of each enemy it kills. It gains a star at 1.5×, 3× and 5× its own cost (Veteran, Elite, Heroic), and one big kill can skip ranks. Each star gives +20% damage dealt and 20% less damage taken, so three stars hit 60% harder and take 37.5% less. Heroic units also heal 1% of their health per second. Stars show above the unit.

Tech

Unity architecture

  • Unity 6 LTS + URP, C#, New Input System, UI Toolkit for menus and HUD.
  • Data-driven content. Every nation, unit, building, weapon and armor type is a ScriptableObject, so designers balance without touching code.
  • Simulation separated from view. Game logic runs on a fixed 15 Hz tick and the renderer interpolates. This keeps a lockstep multiplayer option open later.
  • Pathfinding. Grid flow fields for group movement plus local avoidance. Unity NavMesh struggles with hundreds of units. A* Pathfinding Project Pro is a paid fallback.
  • Performance. Jobs + Burst for movement, targeting and visibility, aiming for 300 units at 60 fps.
  • Fog of war. Visibility grid computed in the sim and uploaded as a texture that a ground shader samples.
  • AI. Per-nation build orders as ScriptableObjects, plus a utility AI that chooses between expand, defend, harass and all-in.
  • Addressables for per-nation assets, so each faction loads only what it uses.

Foundation

Base building & economy

Sidebar build queue, placement grid, power, ore fields, harvesters, refinery, oil derricks.

M2 · WEEKS 6–8

Combat

Weapons, projectiles, damage table, death and wrecks, veterancy. Atlantic Alliance in greybox.

M3 · WEEKS 9–11

Skirmish shell

Lobby, map loading, fog of war, minimap, win and lose, Red Star Union added.

M4 · WEEKS 12–15

Skirmish AI

Build orders, harvester management, attack waves, 4 difficulty levels. MVP: playable 1v1 vs AI.

M5 · WEEKS 16–21

All five nations

Jade Dynasty, Sand Scorpion, Nova Syndicate, superweapons, balance pass.

M6 · WEEKS 16–24

Art, VFX, audio

Models, explosions, per-nation announcer voice ("Unit ready", "Construction complete"), music.

LATER

Beyond skirmish

Campaign, online multiplayer (lockstep), map editor, replays.
